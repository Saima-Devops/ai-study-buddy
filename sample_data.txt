**Biology** is the scientific study of **life and living organisms**. 

The word comes from two Greek words:
* **"Bios"** meaning *life*
* **"Logos"** meaning *study* or *knowledge*

Biologists study how living things work, how they interact with each other and their environment, how they grow and reproduce, and how they have evolved over time.

---

### What Makes Something "Alive"?
Biology defines a living organism based on several shared characteristics. All living things generally:
1. **Have cells:** Made of one or more microscopic units called cells.
2. **Reproduce:** Can create new life (offspring).
3. **Use energy (Metabolism):** Consume nutrients to fuel their activities (like plants using sunlight or animals eating food).
4. **Maintain balance (Homeostasis):** Keep their internal conditions stable (like humans sweating to cool down).
5. **Grow and develop:** Change and mature over their lifespan.
6. **Respond to their environment:** React to external stimuli (like a plant growing toward the light).
7. **Evolve:** Adapt over generations to survive.

---

### Major Branches of Biology
Because the study of life is so vast, biology is divided into many sub-fields, including:

* **Zoology:** The study of animals.
* **Botany:** The study of plants.
* **Microbiology:** The study of microscopic organisms like bacteria and viruses.
* **Genetics:** The study of heredity and DNA (how traits are passed down).
* **Ecology:** The study of how organisms interact with each other and their habitats.
* **Anatomy & Physiology:** The study of body structures and how they function.
* **Evolutionary Biology:** The study of how species change and adapt over millions of years.

---

### Why is Biology Important?
* **Medicine & Health:** It helps us understand diseases, develop vaccines, and understand how the human body works.
* **Food & Agriculture:** It teaches us how to breed healthier crops and livestock to feed the world.
* **Environmental Protection:** It helps us understand ecosystems so we can protect endangered species and fight climate change.
